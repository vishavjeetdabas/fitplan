## 📈 PROGRESSIVE OVERLOAD

1. **Week 1-2:** Learn the form. Use moderate weight. Hit the top of rep range.
2. **Week 3-4:** Once you hit top reps for ALL sets, increase weight by 2.5 kg (upper) or 5 kg (lower).
3. **Track everything** in a notebook or app.
4. **If you fail minimum reps** after adding weight, stay at that weight until you can.
